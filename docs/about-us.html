Our story

Family-owned. Owner-led. Honest.

Climate Control Solutions is a family-owned and operated heating and air conditioning company based in Kyle, Texas. Our owner and service technician, Marcus, has more than a decade of experience in the HVAC industry and a passion for helping neighbors keep their systems running smoothly.

Every member of our team is licensed, insured and trained. We repair, replace and maintain AC and heating systems of every major brand, and we install whole-home air quality products for cleaner indoor air.

We earn our reviews the simple way: we show up on time, keep you updated, find the real problem and tell you the truth about it. If a repair will give you more good years, we'll recommend the repair. That's why so many of our 662 Google reviews use the word honest.
