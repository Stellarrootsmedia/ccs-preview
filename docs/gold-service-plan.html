Spring air conditioning visit

Before Central Texas heat sets in, we get your AC ready and watch for early signs of wear that cause midsummer breakdowns.

  • AC system inspection and deep cleaning
  • Refrigerant check and performance test
  • Thermostat calibration and control check
  • Electrical, drain and condenser safety checks

Fall heating visit

When nights cool down, your furnace or heat pump needs to be safe and ready. We focus on reliability and safety.

  • Furnace or heat pump inspection
  • Burner, ignition and safety controls check
  • Filter replacement, if provided by the homeowner
  • Airflow check and carbon monoxide safety test

Priority service

How priority service works

Gold members jump to the front of the line. When you call during a busy week or after a sudden weather change, your request is flagged for the next available appointment. If you need after-hours help, your plan speeds up the next service window so we can get you comfortable sooner.

Repairs found during maintenance are explained clearly, and your 10% discount is applied right on the ticket.

Tip: book your spring visit before late April and your fall visit before early November to beat the seasonal rush.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How often should my AC and heater be serviced?

At least once a year for each. Ideally that means an AC tune-up in spring before the Central Texas heat and a heating check in fall before the first cold front. Our Gold Service Plan covers both visits.

How do I book an appointment?

Call (512) 960-5282 or use the Book online button to pick a time. If you'd rather talk first, call and we'll answer your questions before you schedule.

Do you offer emergency HVAC service?

Yes. Emergency heating and air conditioning service is available 24/7. Call (512) 960-5282, day or night.
