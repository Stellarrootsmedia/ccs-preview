Indoor Air Quality

Signs your indoor air needs help

  • Allergies are worse at home

    Dust, pollen and pet dander circulating through your ducts can keep symptoms going indoors.

  • Dust comes back fast

    If surfaces get dusty soon after cleaning, your filtration probably isn't keeping up.

  • Musty or stale smells

    Lingering odors can point to moisture, mold or poor ventilation.

  • Pets, smokers or recent remodeling

    These all add particles that standard filters miss.

Our approach

How we improve your air

We'll look at your HVAC system and your concerns, then recommend the right fit, from upgraded whole-home filtration to air purifiers that capture fine particles like mold spores and pollen.

Because these products work with your existing heating and cooling system, they clean the air throughout the house, not just one room.

Cleaner air also means a cleaner system. Less dust on coils and fans helps your equipment run more efficiently.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can you help with allergies or dusty air in my home?

Yes. We install whole-home air quality products such as upgraded filtration and air purifiers that capture dust, pollen, pet dander and mold spores. We'll look at your system and recommend what fits your home.

How often should my AC and heater be serviced?

At least once a year for each. Ideally that means an AC tune-up in spring before the Central Texas heat and a heating check in fall before the first cold front. Our Gold Service Plan covers both visits.

Which brands do you work on?

All major brands and models, including Carrier, Bryant, Lennox, Goodman, American Standard and York. Our recent installs include Bryant, Carrier and Goodman systems.
