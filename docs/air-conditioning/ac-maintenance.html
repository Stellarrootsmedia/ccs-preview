AC Maintenance

What a tune-up covers

  • Inspection and cleaning

    We inspect the system and clear built-up dirt and debris from the coils so it can release heat efficiently.

  • Refrigerant and performance check

    We check refrigerant levels and test how well the system is actually cooling.

  • Thermostat and controls

    We calibrate the thermostat and confirm the controls are working correctly.

  • Electrical, drain and condenser safety

    We check connections, capacitors and the condensate drain to prevent hard starts, leaks and fire hazards.

Our approach

Why yearly maintenance pays off

Spring is the best time for AC maintenance, before the long hot season starts. Small issues like a weak capacitor or a dirty coil are cheap to fix in April and expensive to fix in August.

Regular maintenance helps your system run more efficiently, last longer and keep your warranty in good standing.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How often should my AC and heater be serviced?

At least once a year for each. Ideally that means an AC tune-up in spring before the Central Texas heat and a heating check in fall before the first cold front. Our Gold Service Plan covers both visits.

My AC isn't blowing cold air. What should I check first?

Make sure the thermostat is set to Cool and the set temperature is below room temperature, check that the air filter isn't clogged, and confirm supply and return vents aren't blocked. Check the breaker for the outdoor unit too. If it still isn't cooling, turn the system off so a frozen coil or failing part doesn't get worse, then call us at (512) 960-5282.
